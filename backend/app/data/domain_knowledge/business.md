# ENTERPRISE STRATEGY, UNIT ECONOMICS & B2B BENCHMARKS: BUSINESS DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE BASE
Reference Standards: Bessemer Venture Partners (BVP) State of the Cloud, Y Combinator Startup Financial Advisory, Indian DPDP Act 2023

## 1. Quantitative Unit Economics & Payback Formulas
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Payback Formula:
  - Formula: `CAC Payback (Months) = Fully Loaded CAC / (Monthly ARPU * Gross Margin %)`
  - Benchmark Thresholds by Tier:
    - B2C / Self-Serve Product-Led Growth (ACV < $1k USD / ₹80k INR): CAC payback must be under 6 to 12 months due to high voluntary churn.
    - Mid-Market B2B (ACV $10k–$50k USD / ₹8L–₹40L INR): CAC payback must be under 12 to 18 months.
    - Enterprise Contracts (ACV > $50k USD / ₹40L+ INR): CAC payback of 18 to 24 months is healthy given high contract values and multi-year lock-ins. Payback > 24 months signals capital-inefficient go-to-market motions requiring equity subsidization.
- Lifetime Value to CAC (LTV:CAC):
  - Formula: `LTV = (ARPU * Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate`. Target `LTV:CAC >= 3.0x`.
  - An LTV:CAC < 2.0x signals flawed pricing, poor retention, or excessive acquisition spend.
  - An LTV:CAC > 5.0x indicates under-investment in marketing and distribution expansion.
- Gross Margins & Compute COGS:
  - Software Gross Margin benchmark: 75% to 85%+.
  - For AI-native SaaS, cloud compute and LLM API token inference costs (Cost of Goods Sold) must not exceed 12% to 15% of Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR).

## 2. Capital Efficiency, Runway & The Rule of 40
- The Burn Multiple (David Sacks / Craft Ventures):
  - Formula: `Burn Multiple = Net Burn / Net New ARR Added`
  - Rating Scale:
    - < 1.0x: Top Decile / Amazing capital efficiency.
    - 1.0x to 1.5x: Good / VC-investable.
    - 1.5x to 2.0x: Suspect / High cash burn rate.
    - > 2.0x: Alarming / Unsustainable cash hemorrhage.
- The Rule of 40:
  - Formula: `Year-over-Year Revenue Growth Rate % + Free Cash Flow Margin % >= 40%`.
  - Growth-stage companies trade profit margin for revenue velocity; late-stage companies transition to cash generation.
- Minimum Survivable Runway:
  - 18 to 24 months of verified cash buffer to insulate against macroeconomic downturns or elongated enterprise procurement cycles.

## 3. Indian Corporate Governance, Tax Withholding & DPDP Statutory Realities
- Indian Enterprise Statutory Compliance:
  - Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023): Mandatory explicit consent records, appointment of Data Protection Officer (DPO), and strict financial penalties up to ₹250 Crore for data breach negligence. Personal data must not be transferred to blacklisted countries.
  - Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) Withholding:
    - Section 194J of Income Tax Act: 2% TDS on technical services / software royalty, 10% on professional services. Enterprise enterprise cash collections must account for 2%–10% client withholding at source.
    - Section 194C: 1%–2% contractor payments.
  - GST Compliance: 18% GST mandatory on software services in India, requiring GSTIN invoice matching for client Input Tax Credit (ITC) eligibility.
- Procurement Realities:
  - Indian domestic corporate willingness-to-pay is value-constrained compared to US/EMEA; enterprise software must either directly generate revenue (GTM) or deliver quantifiable headcount / payroll efficiency.

## 4. Defensibility, Pricing Models & Anti-Shelfware Adoption
- Pricing Models:
  - Per-Seat vs Value / Consumption: Per-seat pricing causes seat-sharing and hinders viral adoption. Hybrid pricing (base platform fee + volume/usage tiers) aligns revenue with customer expansion.
  - Paid Proof of Concept (POC): Free POCs drag out for 6–9 months without executive commitment. Structured 30–60 day paid POCs with predefined numerical success metrics guarantee enterprise conversion.
- Anti-Shelfware Adoption:
  - 40%+ of enterprise SaaS turns into shelfware within 12 months. Weekly Active Usage (WAU/MAU) and integration into daily workflows (Slack, Jira, ERP) are the primary leading indicators of Net Revenue Retention (NRR > 110%).
