# CORPORATE FINANCE, CAPITAL ALLOCATION & TREASURY STANDARDS: FINANCE KNOWLEDGE BASE
Reference Standards: CFA Institute Corporate Finance Standards, Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Directions on Prudential Norms, International Valuation Standards (IVS)

## 1. Capital Allocation, Hurdle Rates & Hurdle Rate Benchmarks
- Internal Rate of Return (IRR) vs Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC):
  - Formula: `WACC = (E/V * Re) + (D/V * Rd * (1 - Tc))` where `Re = Rf + Beta * ERP`.
  - Target IRR Hurdle Rate Matrix:
    - Core Infrastructure & Asset-Backed Capex: Minimum hurdle IRR = 12% to 15%.
    - Growth Private Equity & Mid-Market Expansion: Minimum hurdle IRR = 20% to 25%.
    - Early-Stage Venture Capital / High Risk: Minimum hurdle IRR = 30% to 35%+ (net of portfolio write-offs).
  - Modified Internal Rate of Return (MIRR): For capital projects with reinvestment assumptions, standard IRR overstates returns. Use MIRR assuming cost-of-capital reinvestment rate.
- Net Present Value (NPV) & Payback Horizon:
  - Any capital allocation with negative NPV at company WACC must be rejected regardless of strategic optimism.
  - Discounted Payback Period: Maximum acceptable payback horizon for discretionary enterprise tech/capex is 36 months.

## 2. Leverage Capacity, Debt Service Coverage & Solvency Covenants
- Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR):
  - Formula: `DSCR = Net Operating Income (EBITDA) / Total Debt Service (Principal Repayment + Interest)`.
  - Prudential Banking Standards:
    - DSCR < 1.15x: High default distress, violates standard banking covenants, triggers cash sweep.
    - DSCR 1.25x to 1.50x: Healthy minimum threshold for corporate debt underwriting.
    - DSCR > 2.0x: Conservative balance sheet with debt capacity buffer.
- Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR):
  - Formula: `ICR = EBIT / Interest Expense`. Minimum safe baseline is 3.0x.
- Net Debt to EBITDA Ratio:
  - Benchmark: `Net Debt / EBITDA <= 2.5x` for non-cyclical enterprises; `Net Debt / EBITDA <= 1.5x` for cyclical industries. Exceeding 3.5x exposes the company to rating downgrades.

## 3. Working Capital Optimization & Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)
- The Cash Conversion Cycle Formula:
  - Formula: `CCC = Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO) + Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) - Days Payable Outstanding (DPO)`.
  - Target: Enterprise B2B should target `CCC <= 45 to 60 days`. Negative CCC (Dell/Amazon model) indicates suppliers fund working capital.
- Working Capital Ratio / Current Ratio:
  - `Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities`. Target `1.33x to 1.75x`. Below 1.1x risks liquidity freeze on sudden vendor demands.
- Liquidity Buffers & Cash Drag:
  - Minimum Treasury Liquidity: Minimum 90 days of operational fixed burn maintained in overnight liquid mutual funds or short-dated government T-Bills. Excessive cash buffer (> 36 months) without dividend or reinvestment creates ROIC drag.

## 4. Valuation, Cap Table Dilution & M&A Due Diligence
- Pre-Money vs Post-Money Equity Dilution:
  - Formula: `Post-Money Valuation = Pre-Money Valuation + New Capital Injected`.
  - Investor Ownership % = `New Capital Injected / Post-Money Valuation`.
  - ESOP Expansion Carve-out: Unallocated ESOP pools created "pre-round" dilute existing founders 100%, not the incoming investors. Standard pool benchmark: 10% to 15%.
- Secondary Share Discounts & Liquidity Haircuts:
  - Unlisted / Private secondary transactions command an illiquidity haircut of 20% to 35% relative to the last primary round valuation.
- Quality of Earnings (QoE) Adjustments:
  - Standard EBITDA must be adjusted for non-recurring expenses, related-party transactions, capitalized R&D, and aggressive revenue recognition (ASC 606 / Ind AS 115).
