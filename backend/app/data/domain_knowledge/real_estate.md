# REAL ESTATE, INFRASTRUCTURE CAPEX & LAND ACQUISITION STANDARDS: REAL ESTATE KNOWLEDGE BASE
Reference Standards: Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act 2016 (RERA), Transfer of Property Act 1882, Indian Stamp Act 1899, National Building Code (NBC) 2016

## 1. Zoning, FSI/FAR & Physical Feasibility Metrics
- Floor Space Index (FSI) / Floor Area Ratio (FAR):
  - Formula: `FSI = Total Built-up Area / Total Plot Area`.
  - Premium FSI & TDR (Transferable Development Rights): Additional FSI purchasing formulas mandated by municipal corporations (e.g. BMC, BBMP, DDA) require budgeting 40%–60% of Ready Reckoner / Circle rate per sqft.
  - Setbacks, Ground Coverage & Height Clearances:
    - High-rise commercial thresholds (> 15 meters) require mandatory 6m–9m fire tender setbacks.
    - Ground coverage ceiling: Typically capped at 40%–50% for commercial plots to satisfy open space and rainwater harvesting norms.
- Environmental & Infrastructure Clearances:
  - State Environmental Impact Assessment Authority (SEIAA): Mandatory Prior Environmental Clearance for built-up areas >= 20,000 sq meters (MoEFCC EIA Notification 2006). Violating this threshold halts construction and triggers National Green Tribunal (NGT) demolition risk.
  - Right-of-Way (RoW) & Road Width: Access road width < 12 meters (40 feet) legally disqualifies commercial high-density development in most urban master plans.

## 2. Title Succession, Encumbrance & Legal Due Diligence
- 30-Year Chain of Title Verification:
  - Mandatory search across Sub-Registrar records for minimum 30 consecutive years to ensure uninterrupted marketable title succession.
  - Mother Deed & Revenue Records: Verification of RTC (Record of Rights, Tenancy, and Crops), Patta/Chitta, Mutation Extracts, and Jamabandi.
  - Coparcenary & Ancestral Rights: Under Hindu Succession (Amendment) Act 2005, daughters possess equal coparcenary rights by birth. Acquisition of ancestral property without explicit written partition deed and consent of all legal heirs exposes buyer to partition lawsuits.
- Non-Encumbrance Certificate (EC) & Sub-Registrar Liens:
  - Form 15 Non-Encumbrance Certificate mandatory for the full 30-year search window to confirm absence of registered mortgages, court attachments, or lis pendens (Section 52, Transfer of Property Act).
- Agricultural Land Conversion (Change of Land Use - CLU):
  - Section 143/80 of Revenue Codes: Agricultural land cannot be developed for commercial/industrial purposes without formal CLU order and conversion fees. Non-farmers acquiring agricultural land in prohibited states (e.g., Karnataka Section 79A/B historical scrutiny, Maharashtra Section 63) invalidates sale.

## 3. Commercial Transaction Structures & RERA Statutory Escrows
- Joint Development Agreement (JDA) vs Outright Sale:
  - Outright Land Purchase: 100% upfront capital commitment, clean operational control, high cash drag, zero revenue leakage.
  - Joint Development Agreement (JDA / Revenue Share):
    - Standard commercial sharing ratio: 55:45 to 65:35 (Developer: Landowner).
    - Power of Attorney (PoA): Specific irrevocable PoA strictly limited to development activities; execution PoA must not transfer ownership title prior to unit conveyance.
- RERA Statutory Escrow Compliance:
  - Section 4(2)(l)(D) of RERA Act, 2016: 70% of all customer collections must be deposited into a dedicated scheduled bank escrow account. Withdrawals are permitted strictly in proportion to percentage of project completion certified by an Engineer, Architect, and practicing Chartered Accountant.
  - Diversion of project funds constitutes a criminal offence under Section 59/60 with penalties up to 10% of total project cost or imprisonment.
- Stamp Duty & Circle Rate Discrepancy Risk:
  - Section 50C & 56(2)(x) of Income Tax Act: If land transaction value is lower than the government Circle Rate / Ready Reckoner value by > 10%, the delta is deemed taxable income for both buyer and seller.
  - Stamp duty typically ranges 5%–7% of registered transaction value plus 1% municipal registration surcharge.
